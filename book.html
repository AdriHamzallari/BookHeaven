The Silent Echo

by Jane Harper
⭐ 4.5 (1,284 reviews) $19.99

In the quiet coastal town of Port Agatha, a woman's body is found on the beach at dawn. No identification, no signs of struggle — only a single, cryptic note clutched in her hand. Detective Kieran Harper returns to his childhood hometown to investigate, only to uncover secrets that have been buried for decades. As the past and present collide, Kieran must confront the ghosts of his own history before the killer strikes again.

A gripping literary mystery that weaves family drama, psychological tension, and the haunting beauty of the Australian coast. Perfect for fans of slow-burn thrillers and atmospheric storytelling.
